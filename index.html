FAQ

Common questions

You share your unique sync code with a caregiver. In this demo, everything runs locally in your browser and the sync is simulated — but the flow mirrors a real product: adherence events, missed doses, refill warnings and severe side-effect entries are pushed to every linked caregiver according to their alert level, with a last-sync timestamp you can verify.

Every scheduled dose is scored: taken counts 100%, skipped counts 0%, and a dose left untouched past its grace window counts as missed (0%). Doses that are still upcoming or inside their reminder window are excluded so your score is never unfairly penalised. The 7-day chart and the streak use the same rules.

CareSync raises a missed-dose alert in your dashboard and the caregiver feed, and the AI companion walks you through the safe options: take it as soon as you remember if it's still close to the scheduled time, or skip and resume the normal schedule — never double up. Always follow the specific guidance from your doctor or the medication leaflet.

Each medication tracks pills remaining and a refill size. Every dose you mark as taken decrements the count, and the card shows how many days of supply remain based on your daily dose frequency. When pills fall to 20% of the refill size (or fewer than 3 days of supply), the medication is flagged as “Refill due” on your dashboard and in the alert feed.
